## 1. Latest reported results: Q1 FY2027 (quarter ended Aug 31, 2026, reported Sep 10, 2026) and full FY2026 (ended May 31, 2026, reported Jun 10, 2026)

### Takeaway
The latest reported quarter is **Q1 FY27**, released Sep 10, 2026. Revenue was ~$19.35B (+30% YoY), and OCI revenue more than doubled to $7.4B (+121%). Non-GAAP EPS was $1.92 (+30%) and GAAP EPS $1.56 (+55%). Free cash flow was still negative, about -$5.4B in the quarter, on ~$28.5B of capex. FY26 revenue was $67.4B (+17%), with capex of $55.7B and FCF of -$23.7B. Q2 FY27 (Sep–Nov 2026) has not been reported yet.

## 2. RPO / backlog: size, growth, concentration, conversion

### Takeaway
RPO was **$664B at Aug 31, 2026**, up $209B YoY. The sequential gain was only ~$26B, compared with the +$85B jump in Q4 FY26 to $638B. Oracle does not disclose customer concentration, but rating agencies and press estimate that OpenAI is roughly half. Only ~12% of the May 31 RPO was expected to be recognized within 12 months. Management now says "around half" converts within 36 months.

## 3. Capex plans, financing, debt, credit ratings, CDS

### Takeaway
FY27 gross capex is guided at **~$90–95B**, with net cash capex of **no more than ~$70B** after $20–25B of customer prepayments and other offsets. Oracle completed a $20B ATM equity program.
