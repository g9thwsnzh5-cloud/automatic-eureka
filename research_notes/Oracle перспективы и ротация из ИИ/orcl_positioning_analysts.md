## 2. Short interest and public short theses

### Takeaway
Exchange-reported short interest in ORCL is modest: about 47.5M shares, about 2.5% of float, about 1.7 days to cover as of the 2026-10-15 settlement. That is roughly double the level of a year earlier, though it slipped ~2% in the latest period. Most of the bearish expression runs through credit (CDS) and options rather than outright stock shorts. Michael Burry's January-2027 puts and his depreciation critique are the best-known public bear position. He took profit on half in July 2026 but kept the other half.

### Cited Findings
- **Latest short interest, settlement 2026-10-15:** about 47.5M shares short, down 1.99% from the prior period, with 1.66 days to cover. MarketBeat shows short interest at 2.55% of float. — [Options Analysis Suite (FINRA bi-monthly)](https://www.optionsanalysissuite.com/stocks/orcl/short-interest), [MarketBeat ORCL short interest](https://www.marketbeat.com/stocks/NYSE/ORCL/short-interest/)
- **Prior data point, 2026-08-31:** 44.6M shares short, which one source reports as 1.55% of float with 2.32 days to cover. The percent-of-float figure conflicts with MarketBeat's ~2.5%, probably because the sources use different float definitions; Ellison's large stake makes free float smaller than shares outstanding. StockAnalysis shows 2.60% of float and 1.42 days to cover, but that data is stale (~116 days old). — [SmartInvestorsDaily](https://smartinvestorsdaily.com/stocks/NASDAQ/ORCL/short-interest), [StockAnalysis statistics](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/orcl/statistics/)

- The new $10B equity issue (86.2M shares at $116, closing 2026-11-04) is likely to generate hedging and arbitrage short activity around the deal. The next FINRA prints, for the 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-14 settlements, may show a rise in short interest.

## 3. Credit market view: CDS, bond spreads, bank hedging, ratings

### Takeaway
The credit market is the most bearish venue on Oracle. Five-year CDS reached record highs of about 288 bp around 2026-10-15, and one unconfirmed report puts it at ~331 bp on 2026-10-21. It was about 292-296 bp on 2026-11-02, up from about 144 bp at the start of 2026 and about 57 bp before the AI-capex cycle. Oracle's investment-grade bonds trade wider than the average BB junk bond. S&P is at BBB-/Stable (cut 2026-07-09), Moody's is at Baa2/Negative and Fitch is at BBB. Much of the CDS buying is attributed to banks hedging their data-center loan exposure, while Saba Capital has reportedly been selling protection.

- **Ratings:**
  - S&P cut Oracle from BBB to BBB- (one notch above junk) on 2026-07-09 with a Stable outlook, and cut the short-term rating from A-2 to A-3, citing mounting debt and sharply negative cash flow ([Briefs.co](https://www.briefs.co/news/s-p-downgrades-oracle-s-bond-rating-to-one-notch-above-junk/), [Computing.co.uk](https://www.computing.co.uk/news/2026/ai/oracle-under-pressure-after-ratings-downgrade-over-ai-expansion), [Bloomberg 2026-07-16](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-16/oracle-risks-falling-behind-in-ai-race-as-spending-binge-bites)).
  - A 2026 Oracle FWP pricing term sheet lists expected ratings of Baa2 (Negative) at Moody's, BBB- (Stable) at S&P and BBB (Stable) at Fitch ([SEC FWP](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001341439/000119312526443918/d87441dfwp.htm)).
  - Oracle's FY2026 10-K warns that downgrades could raise commercial-paper and short-term funding costs and affect data-center leases ([10-K FY2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001341439/000119312526277521/orcl-20260531.htm)).
- **$20B capital raise, filed 2026-11-02:** $10B of common stock (86.2M shares at $116, about an 11% discount to the prior close) and $10B of senior notes, both closing 2026-11-04. Proceeds are for general corporate purposes including capex and debt repayment. Oracle said it would cap additional capital-market funding for the FY2027-2030 investment cycle at this $20B. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/02/oracle-files-to-sell-20-billion-in-new-stock-bonds-to-finance-ai-buildout.html), [Yahoo Finance "ORCL inches higher after pricing"](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/orcl-inches-higher-pricing-20b-052422978.html), [TipRanks](https://tipranks.com/news/oracle-stock-trickles-down-as-tech-giant-plans-20-billion-fundraise-for-ai-buildout)

### Inferences
- Issuing equity alongside debt reads as a credit-defensive move to protect the IG rating; Oracle has said it will defend IG. It is dilutive for equity holders (~3% more shares), but supportive at the margin for bondholders. Watch whether CDS tightens after the deal closes on 2026-11-04.
- Credit pricing at BB levels against a BBB-/Baa2 rating means the market is already pricing a high chance of a fallen-angel event. Moody's negative outlook is the most likely next trigger.
- A meaningful part of the CDS widening is technical, from bank hedging of construction loans. That makes CDS an imperfect pure "default probability" signal, though it still raises Oracle's funding costs.

### Gaps
- I found no Fitch rating action in 2026. The BBB/Stable comes from a single term sheet.
- Moody's negative outlook date and any review-for-downgrade status are unconfirmed.
- The 331 bp (2026-10-21) figure is single-source.
- Spreads on specific ORCL bonds (e.g. the 2055s) and the pricing of the new $10B notes were not found.
