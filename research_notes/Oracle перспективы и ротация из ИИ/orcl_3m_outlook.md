## 1. Upcoming events and consensus for Q2 FY2027 (quarter ends ~Nov 30, 2026)

### Takeaway
Oracle's Q2 FY27 report is expected in mid-December 2026. Calendars list Dec 9, 10 or 14; I found no confirmed date. Oracle's guidance is revenue growth of +30–34% (about $20.9–21.5B) and non-GAAP EPS of $1.85–1.93 in USD. Consensus sits near the midpoint: about $21.1–21.2B revenue and $1.82–1.89 EPS. Before the print, the market will watch four things: whether Oracle holds its FY27 guide (at least $90B revenue, $90–95B capex), cash burn after the $20B raise closes on Nov 4, Nvidia's report on Nov 17, and OpenAI's funding.
